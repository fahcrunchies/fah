What The FAH ?

FAH was born from a simple idea — to bring the joy of bold Indian flavours into a modern cult snacking experience.

We believe some of the best food memories begin with Ex's😉, Bold snacks: shared with mansion family, enjoyed with magic friends, or savoured during a small break from the every monks .

FAH takes that familiar spirit and gives it a contemporary, premium expression, created for the way people snack today.

Our first creation, FAH Crunchies, brings together bold Desi Masala flavour and a satisfying crunch in a convenient packs.

FAH — Find All Happiness is more than a name. It represents those little moments that make an ordinary man feel like Royal stag — a party simbha, a shared Rhino, a familiar bira, and a moment to be young.

Our vision is to build FAH into a brand that celebrates India's bold snacking cult while creating products that naturally belong for exicting magic moments.

Because happiness doesn't always have to be a Godfather. Sometimes, it's just one good crunch.
